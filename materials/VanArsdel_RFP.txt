Request for Proposal (RFP)

Issued by: VanArsdel, Ltd.

Project: Smart Energy Management Solution for Hospitality Properties

1. Introduction & Background

VanArsdel, Ltd. is a leading OEM specializing in advanced solutions for the hospitality industry. We are seeking proposals from qualified vendors to provide a comprehensive smart energy management solution for our portfolio of hotels and resorts. The goal is to optimize energy usage, reduce operational costs, and support sustainability initiatives across our properties.

2. Project Objectives

Deploy a scalable energy management platform for hotels and resorts.

Integrate IoT sensors, analytics, and automated controls for HVAC, lighting, and plug loads.

Achieve measurable energy savings and support sustainability certifications (e.g., LEED, ENERGY STAR).

Enable real-time monitoring, reporting, and predictive maintenance.

3. Scope of Work

Supply and install all necessary hardware and software.

Integrate with existing property management and building management systems.

Provide training and ongoing support for property staff.

Ensure compliance with relevant industry standards and local regulations.

4. Technical Requirements

Support for wireless IoT sensors (temperature, occupancy, humidity, light, CO2).

Cloud-based management portal with mobile access.

Secure communications (TLS/SSL), data encryption, and ISO 27001 compliance.

Open API for third-party integrations.

Automated demand response and sustainability reporting features.

5. Vendor Qualifications

Demonstrated experience in deploying energy management solutions for hospitality clients.

References from at least three similar projects.

Ability to provide ongoing support and maintenance.
