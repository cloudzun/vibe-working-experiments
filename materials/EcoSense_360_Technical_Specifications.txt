1. Product Overview

EcoSense 360 is Fabrikam’s next-generation smart energy management platform designed for hotels, resorts, and hospitality venues. It integrates advanced IoT sensors, AI-driven analytics, and cloud-based controls to optimize energy usage, reduce costs, and support sustainability goals.

2. System Architecture

Core Components:

EcoSense 360 Hub (central controller)

Wireless IoT Sensors (temperature, occupancy, humidity, light)

Smart Thermostats and Lighting Controllers

Cloud Management Portal (web/mobile)

API Gateway for third-party integrations

Network Protocols:

Zigbee, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth LE

Secure TLS/SSL encryption for all device communications

Supported Platforms:

Windows, iOS, Android (mobile apps)

Web browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari)

3. Key Features & Capabilities

Real-Time Monitoring: Live dashboards for energy consumption, occupancy, and environmental metrics.

Automated Controls: AI-driven scheduling and adaptive control of HVAC, lighting, and plug loads.

Integration: Native support for major hotel PMS, Open RESTful API, BACnet/IP and Modbus compatibility.

Reporting & Analytics: Customizable reports, predictive maintenance alerts, ROI calculators.

Sustainability: LEED and ENERGY STAR compliance support, automated demand response participation.
