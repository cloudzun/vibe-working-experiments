EcoSense 360 Customer Case Study

Transforming Energy Management at the Grand Vista Resort

Background

The Grand Vista Resort is a 200-room, midscale property located in Orlando, Florida, catering to both leisure and business travelers. Facing rising energy costs, sustainability mandates, and the need to enhance guest comfort, the resort’s management sought a modern solution to optimize energy usage and support their green initiatives.

Challenges

High and unpredictable energy expenses (over $250,000 annually)

Inefficient HVAC and lighting controls leading to wasted energy

Difficulty meeting sustainability certification requirements (LEED, ENERGY STAR)

Need for real-time monitoring and actionable insights

Solution: EcoSense 360 Implementation

In early 2025, Grand Vista Resort partnered with Fabrikam to deploy the EcoSense 360 energy management platform. The solution included:

Wireless IoT sensors for temperature, occupancy, humidity, and lighting

Smart thermostats and automated lighting controls in all guest rooms and public areas

Cloud-based management portal for real-time monitoring and analytics

Integration with the property’s existing PMS and BMS systems

Sustainability reporting tools for certification compliance

Results & Impact

Energy Savings & ROI

Achieved a 32% reduction in annual energy costs (saving $80,000/year)

Typical payback period: 2.5 years

Operational Efficiency

Automated controls reduced manual intervention by 80%

Predictive maintenance alerts minimized equipment downtime

Sustainability & Compliance

Earned LEED Silver and ENERGY STAR certifications within 12 months

Reduced carbon footprint by 110 metric tons/year

Guest Experience

Personalized room comfort settings increased guest satisfaction scores by 18%

Real-time occupancy-based controls improved overall comfort and reduced complaints
